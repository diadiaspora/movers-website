    Good to Know

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. We carry liability and cargo insurance. Optional full-value protection is available for high-value items.

    How far in advance should I book?

    Two to three weeks is ideal. End-of-month and summer weekends fill up fastest, so book early for those dates.

    Do you charge for travel time?

    Billing starts when the crew arrives and ends when unloading is done. There is a flat $60 trip fee for moves over 25 miles.

    What can't you move?

    We can't transport hazardous materials such as propane, gasoline, paint, or ammunition. Plants and perishables are moved at your own risk.

    How do I pay?

    We accept all major cards and bank transfers. A 20% deposit holds your date, and the balance is due at the end of the move.
